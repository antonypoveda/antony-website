This was a week with a lot of talking to other teams. Mostly good talking. I like talking, it makes the work better.

## This week

* We did a show and tell on the first discovery sprint for the wider Capability team, and are setting up calls with colleagues to talk about their current service offers.
* We've recruited participants from the profession for user research interviews, and made progress with being able to do research with people outside of government.
* We caught up with another discovery team in GDS looking at an adjacent problem area, which will help us focus our scope.
* I did a session taking our team through the current hypothetical user experience for people looking for career ideas, adapting these [heuristics from the NHS App team](https://mikegallagher.org/posts/design-principles-for-healthcare/).
* We explored some potential strategic framings for our work and started thinking about [theory of change](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/the-analysis-function-theory-of-change-toolkit/).
* We had a couple of really good chats with a new team in our area that should help us operate as one group when we need to work with the Future Civil Service programme.
* Everyone survived the Great MS-DOS Outage of 2026, until the next one.

## Next
* We should be finalising the content changes to go live on the capability framework at the end of the month.
* I'm running the November edition of the [X-Gov Sheffield meetup](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=BXCsy8EC60O0l-ZJLRst2Jre5EtS4GJGqikwhOdA5qpUMENURFIzOUZOQkFDREhGVzVMTFhXMTlCVi4u) on Tuesday.
