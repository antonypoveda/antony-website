A little week with just 3 days of work, so let's keep this snappy.

## This week

This week, I have:
* moved my non-working day so we could take Kid A to Gulliver's Valley with their friends before first ever days of school - such a fun day out and the school start seems to have gone well too, phew
* finished up (almost) all of the documentation following the latest update to the [Digital and Data Profession Capability Framework](https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/)
* planned the content change work for this month's sprint cycle  - with the understanding that things will likely need re-prioritising or pushing back because of site migration
* got back to testing the rebuilt site frontend and our new CMS - getting closer, just a couple of chunky things remaining to work on with the dev, such as search and feedback components
* chatted briefly again with fellow content designers and information architects about 'how to fix all the GDS guidance things' - this involved a convoluted analogy based on the [council scene from LOTR](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AkKzZr9RT0E) which I swear was helpful in the moment
* a couple of sessions thinking deeply about how we might change  'skills' to make things better for the profession and government - great discussions with our team and capability leads from around gov
* more notes and ideas for [my SDinGov session](https://sdingov.net/programme/power-profession-what-happens-when-users-can-vote-change-your-product) - I absolutely have to start putting the slides together next week, or else
* walked the new service owner through every step of the current governance and co-design processes that effectively form our ['change the framework' service](/weeknotes/09-08-2026/#:~:text=Our%202%20core,or%20future%20APIs) - we've started a really detailed diagram, yey!
* struggled with some recruitment and HR fun - nuff said
* checked on signups for the next X-Gov Sheffield meetup, and happily we've already hit our unofficial target - you can still [sign up to join us for breakfast  and coffee on 17 September](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=BXCsy8EC60O0l-ZJLRst2Jre5EtS4GJGqikwhOdA5qpUMENURFIzOUZOQkFDREhGVzVMTFhXMTlCVi4u)

## Next week
